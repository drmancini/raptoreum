The design says what we intend to build. The log says what happened on which day. This says
**what we know**, one line per item, with the regime it was measured in and a status.

**Why the regime column exists.** Nearly every contradiction the cross-check found was two
correct numbers from different conditions presented as one disputed number. A value without its
regime is not a finding.

| regime | what it means |
|---|---|
| `source` | read from the code, not measured |
| `loopback` | one host, Ryzen 9 3900X 12c/24t, peers on loopback |
| `wan-12` | twelve nodes across three continents. **Mostly 4-thread VPS, but the set includes a 24-thread and a 12-thread host**, and **no smartnode features were active** — no quorums, no islocks, no chainlocks — so every `wan-12` figure is an **upper bound** |

| F-37b | **but a rung is still needed** — R-28 retracts the reason it was not | `ConnectBlock` does **not** re-invoke `ContextualCheckBlock`; its own comment says so. `nLockTime` finality, `bad-cb-type` (DIP3 coinbase), `bad-txns-type`, BIP34 height and `bad-txns-oversize` are enforced **only** at accept time, and `CheckBlock` is re-run at connect only when `block.fChecked` is false. | F-38 | `BLOCK_HAVE_BODIES = 256` costs **zero** relaxed assertions | the commitment block is stored like any block, so `BLOCK_HAVE_DATA` stays honest. | F-38b | "on the active chain ⇒ bodies" is **not** an invariant | pruning removes bodies from blocks that stay connected, and the assertion would forbid the design's own steady state — commitments for all of history, bodies for a window. | F-38c | the narrowed guards needed a recovery rule or nothing checked a gap descendant | both the candidacy and the unlinked assertions are skipped for a block above a body gap. | F-39 | both encodings break the criterion's chain, at different links | 0.1's at `HAVE_DATA ⇒ nTx > 0`; the bit encoding at `VALID_TRANSACTIONS ⇒ candidacy` — a block above the tip with all parent data "must be in setBlockIndexCandidates", and selection had just dropped it for having no bodies | `bench-rt` | stands |

| F-42 | not re-stamping `nSequenceId` displaces an equal-work tip | a block whose *commitments* arrived early keeps an earlier id than an equal-work rival that arrived whole, so when its bodies land it displaces an already-connected tip — which unmodified code never does, and which a miner could reserve by announcing commitments early and bodies late. | R-28 | "no rung is needed — `ConnectBlock` re-checks those properties anyway" | **wrong**: `ConnectBlock` does not re-invoke `ContextualCheckBlock`, and the property list came from an enum comment that omits it. 0.1c, 18 Sep |
| F-43 | **the rung sits after the commitment-checkable checks, and they are not trivial** | proof of work, the header-chain rules, **merkle root**, merkle malleation, size, first-tx-is-coinbase, the whole coinbase `CheckTransaction` (including the **founder payment**), coinbase finality and `bad-cb-type`. | F-44 | **three** rules have no connect-time home | `ConnectBlock` re-invokes `CheckBlock` (first statement, before the genesis return and before `fScriptChecks`), so `bad-cb-multiple`, `CheckTransaction` and sigops are covered. `ContextualCheckBlock` is not re-invoked, leaving: `nLockTime` finality; type/version (partly — `nVersion == 3` with an unknown type dies at connect, `nVersion < 3` with a non-zero type and `nVersion > 3` do not); and **`bad-txns-oversize` at 100 kB** (F-44b). | F-44b | the missed rule is the one §1A leans on | `bad-txns-oversize` lives in `policy.h` but is `DoS(100)` on the block path, so it is consensus, and it is gated on `DIP0001Enabled` — true everywhere.
